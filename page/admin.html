        Postcards by text or email

        Make a new Gmail just for Zelda's postcards, turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an app password. Family in the phone book (with their email or mobile number filled in) can then text or email a photo to that address.

        Alerts on your phone (ntfy)

        Install the ntfy app, add a subscription and paste the topic below. You'll get a ping when a postcard arrives. The topic works like a password, so keep it long and random.
